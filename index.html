Drag the sliders

Past the frame.

Everything outside the original portrait is new. Each slider compares Mega Crit's original portrait, at its true position and scale, with the extended full-art card. Focus a slider and use the Left and Right arrow keys to move it.

Layered depth

It leans in.

Each layered card pairs a clean background plate with a cut-out foreground that sits in front of the inset frame and shifts toward your cursor, with a soft shadow and rim light.

This preview moves the shipped layers in your browser. It approximates the in-game shader rather than reproducing it exactly.

BaseLib settings

Switch packs live.

Every character and card type is its own pack. Open the mod's settings from the BaseLib menu and switch any pack off under Card packs. The change shows at once, even on cards already on screen, which get the game's original portrait layout back.

  • Toggle any of the 12 bundled packs, or packs from other mods, on their own.
  • Adjust or turn off parallax depth, enable Reduced Motion, or switch to flat full art without layers.
  • Turn rarity finishes off, fade their strength, or swap finish styles.
  • F8Press F8 while hovering a card to write a local report for bug reports.

Pack format v2

Bring your own cards.

Other mods can ship full art for their own cards without touching Full Art Spire's folder. Put a pack under full_art/<pack>/pack.json inside your mod (or, for a player's own packs, under user://full_art_spire/packs/ in the game's user data folder) and match cards by their full .NET runtime_model type name.

  • A JSON Schema (docs/pack.schema.json) gives editor completion and inline errors.
  • tools/validate_pack.py checks manifest fields, confined paths, layers and GPU textures.
  • Optional requires and min_game_version gates skip packs cleanly when a dependency is missing.
Read the pack format Schema & validator on GitHub
full_art/my_character/pack.json 
{
  "format": "full_art_pack",
  "format_version": 2,
  "id": "author.my_character",
  "name": "My Character",
  "version": "1.0.0",
  "requires": [{ "mod": "my_character_mod" }],
  "cards": [
    {
      "runtime_model": "MyMod.Cards.ExampleCard",
      "art": "art/example_card.png",
      "layers": {
        "background": "layers/example_card_bg.png",
        "foreground_mask": "layers/example_card_mask.png"
      }
    }
  ]
}

Transparency

How it's made.

How the full-art extensions, depth layers and packaged files are produced.

AI-generated extensions & backgrounds

The full-art extensions and depth-layer backgrounds are AI-generated (OpenAI image models), starting from Mega Crit's original card art and reviewed against it.

Re-encoded release images

Released images are re-encoded to save space, which removes the C2PA Content Credentials the image generator embedded.

Cut-outs & edge artifacts

Some foreground cut-outs may show small edge artifacts. If you spot one in game, press F8 while hovering the card to save a local report and share it with a screenshot.
